The Magic Backpack

To fix this, we give Robo a magic backpack. Every time he hears a new word, he looks inside his backpack to remember what you said before.

He adds the new word to the story, and packs it all back into the backpack for the next word.

That magic backpack is called the Hidden State!

The "Squishy Backpack" Problem

In a basic RNN, Robo can usually only remember about 5 to 10 words really clearly. Did you notice the words fading in the previous step? Here is why:

The Squishy Backpack:

Imagine packing a real backpack. If you keep putting more and more things inside, the stuff at the very bottom gets squished and hard to find.

Fading Memories:

As new words keep getting stuffed into the Hidden State loop, the oldest words start to fade away or get "squished" by the new ones. By the time you reach the end of a long story, Robo forgets how it started!

Computer Scientists call this fading memory the
"Vanishing Gradient Problem".
